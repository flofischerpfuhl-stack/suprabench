About.

SupraBench is a community-driven, editorially honest ranking of AI models.

The problem

Every benchmark tells you something — but no benchmark tells you everything, and many tell you almost nothing useful. Public leaderboards reward gaming, contaminated training sets, and benchmarks that everyone has saturated. SupraBench tries to fix that by weighting each score by how trustworthy the underlying benchmark actually is.

The SupraScore

A model's SupraScore is the quality-weighted median of its normalised scores across every benchmark it has been evaluated on. Concretely:

  1. For every (model, bench) pair, take the median of all valid community submissions. This rejects single outliers.
  2. Each bench has a community quality score (0–100) — the average of its four dimensions × 20.
  3. The model's SupraScore is the bench-quality-weighted average of those medians. Higher quality benches count more.

The four bench dimensions

  • Relevance — Does the benchmark measure something useful for real-world model use? High = clearly meaningful task. Low = trivia, toy puzzles, artificial setups.
  • Contamination Resistance — How sure are we the test set wasn't in the training data? High = held-out, rotating, or freshly generated questions. Low = scraped from the public web before model release.
  • Discriminability — Does it actually separate weak from strong models? High = wide score spread between SOTA and baseline. Low = saturated, everyone scores 95–100.
  • Reproducibility — Can two independent runs reach the same conclusion? High = deterministic, well-specified protocol. Low = vague setup, judge-LLM, hand-graded.

Tag voting & community correction

Anything the community can get wrong, the community can correct via voting:

  • Tags — every tag on a model or bench is community-voted. Net positive votes keep it; net non-positive removes it from the canonical set.
  • Existence votes — if a model or bench is fake, a duplicate, or wildly wrong, vote it down. Net −3 hides it from listings (but the page stays accessible for context).
  • Submission votes — every individual score gets up/downvoted; only net-positive scores are counted toward the SupraScore.
  • Quality ratings — anyone signed in can rate a benchmark on the four dimensions. The score is the average across raters.

Re-submitting a model or bench you previously had removed under the same name is blocked. Other users may re-submit it (slug gets a numeric suffix), and the community votes again.
