Why ARC-AGI-3 was added

The official competition describes ARC-AGI-3 as an interactive benchmark in which an agent must explore unfamiliar environments, infer their dynamics and goals, and act efficiently. The active 2026 competition, technical report, and public toolkit make it sufficiently relevant and reproducible to track as its own benchmark.

Controls and learnings

  • The machine-readable batch is in manifest.json.
  • Scores are validated against each benchmark's declared numeric scale.
  • Every accepted score source and new benchmark URL requires stored screenshot evidence.
  • Repeated runs are idempotent; changing a curated value requires an explicit, newer replacement.
  • Future runs must match versions exactly and record localized redirects separately from canonical sources.
